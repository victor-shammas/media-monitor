Unemployment is the primary concern for 41% of the population, and wages have fallen significantly, leading university lecturers to lose nearly 10 salaries on average over three years [[99]](https://sonidogremial.com.ar/javier-milei-buscara-la-reeleccion-en-una-argentina-que-crece-con-menos-trabajo-mas-informalidad-y-menos-poder-adquisitivo/) [[102]](https://www.infogremiales.com.ar/fedun-advierte-que-los-docentes-universitarios-ya-perdieron-94-salarios-durante-el-gobierno-de-javier-milei/). Even an economist favored by **Milei** acknowledged that the current anti-inflationary policies are "recessive" and predict only moderate growth for 2027 [[106]](https://www.eldestapeweb.com/economia/economista-favorito-milei-sincero-fulmino-gobierno-recesivo-20261122221130). Critics within the liberal movement describe **Milei**'s economic model as a "strange mix," with an overvalued currency and high interest rates imposing social costs [[100]](https://desalambrar.com.ar/estamos-trabajando-con-varios-sectores-de-centroderecha-con-la-libertad-avanza-no-queremos-ir-porque-esta-manejada-por-peronistas-de-la-vieja-escuela/).

In Italy, the **Meloni** government's 2027 budget is criticized as a "magic trick" that defers fiscal burdens and passes debt to future administrations [[36]](https://ilmanifesto.it/rinvii-e-riarmo-la-manovra-a-futura-memoria). The **Lega** party's proposal for a building permit "amnesty" using a "silence-is-consent" mechanism is facing resistance from allies and strong opposition, with critics warning of widespread legalization of unauthorized constructions [[40]](https://www.corriere.it/economia/finanza/26_novembre_23/condono-edilizio-come-funziona-il-silenzio-assenso-della-lega-senza-la-risposta-del-comune-in-sei-mesi-via-libera-alla-sanatoria-ff2f3248-e577-4e5b-b869-ba4296afaxlk.shtml) [[58]](https://www.repubblica.it/economia/2026/11/23/news/manovra_condono_edilizio_matteo_salvini-425742720/) [[60]](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/in-edicola/articoli/2026/11/23/babbo-natale-salvini-regala-un-condono-e-fdi-sragiona-su-gaza/8580464/). The budget also includes a notable 26.84% cut to the National Fund for Asylum Policies and Services, which is expected to negatively impact migrant reception capacity [[41]](https://www.mentinfuga.com/il-governo-meloni-taglia-i-fondi-per-le-politiche-e-i-servizi-dasilo/).

This initiative aims to tackle the persistent housing shortage. In Hungary, the **Tisza** government announced a 120 billion forint pothole repair program, criticizing the previous administration's "catastrophic" road network and rescinding a 23,000 billion forint motorway concession to prevent "Hungarian people's money" from flowing to oligarchs [[210]](https://bahir.hu/2026/11/23/120-milliard-forintos-katyumentesito-programot-jelentett-be-magyar-peter/) [[223]](https://www.budapesttimes.hu/hungary/government-launches-120bn-forint-pothole-repair-programme/). These actions highlight a common right-wing strategy of both fiscal austerity and populist spending, often framed as addressing national interests and correcting past misgovernance.

## Transnational Right-Wing Connections and Divisions

The global right-wing landscape reveals both intensifying cooperation and significant internal divisions. The "Patriots for Europe" group, co-chaired by **Fidesz** MEP **Kinga Gál**, is actively working to consolidate right-wing forces in the European Parliament, aiming to create a "huge group" by integrating **Marine Le Pen**'s **National Rally** (Patriots), **Giorgia Meloni**'s **Fratelli d'Italia** (ECR), and the **AfD** (ESN) after the 2029 elections [[200]](https://wisevoter.com/world/2026/11/23/far-right-eu-power-shift-after-2029) [[224]](http://www.bgnes.com/politics/europe-s-far-right-sets-sights-on-reshaping-eu-by-2029) [[343]](https://en.apa.az/europe/politico-eus-far-right-forces-seek-to-remove-ursula-von-der-leyen-from-office-537884) [[349]](https://www.politico.eu/article/far-right-patriots-for-europe-national-election-wins-transform-eu/). This ambitious project seeks to shift the EU's balance of power and curb the European Commission's authority by returning more decision-making to member states [[200]](https://wisevoter.com/world/2026/11/23/far-right-eu-power-shift-after-2029) [[343]](https://en.apa.az/europe/politico-eus-far-right-forces-seek-to-remove-ursula-von-der-leyen-from-office-537884).

However, cracks in existing right-wing alliances are also evident. The defeat of **Viktor Orbán**'s ally **Aleksandar Vucic** in Serbia and the significant losses for **MAGA Republicans** in the US midterm elections have reportedly diminished **Orbán**'s international network and access to funding [[76]](https://www.aljazeera.com/opinions/2026/11/23/is-the-global-far-right-really-in-retreat) [[209]](https://balkaninsight.com/2026/11/23/cash-scandals-arrests-shrinking-alliances-can-hungarys-fidesz-survive/rd/). US President **Donald Trump**'s continued praise for **Vladimir Putin** as "my friend" following their trilateral summit with **Xi Jinping** [[68]](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/donald-trump-cozy-post-vladimir-081730115.html) [[94]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/us-intelligence-chief-defends-trumps-comments-calling-putin-his) has prompted "horror" and public distancing from British right-wing figures, including **Richard Tice** (Reform UK deputy leader), who called **Putin** a "murderous dictator" [[285]](https://twaslnews1.twaslnews.com/8135813/) [[286]](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/11/22/8064082/) [[288]](https://mezha.net/eng/news/mezha-888bb35e91ab080eba3afd21_reform_uk_and/). This indicates a growing divergence on foreign policy positions, even among otherwise aligned parties.

In Poland, **PiS** leader **Jarosław Kaczyński**'s call for a joint right-wing electoral list for the Sejm is met with internal resistance. **Paweł Jabłoński** of **Mateusz Morawiecki**'s **Rozwój Plus** argues against alignment with **Grzegorz Braun** (**Konfederacja Korony Polskiej**), citing Braun's "anti-Polish" stances, including support for NordStream, Holocaust denial, and pro-Russian sentiments, suggesting such an alliance would jeopardize the right's electoral success [[267]](https://wpolityce.pl/polityka/morawiecki-mowi-nie-jablonski-wyjasnia-powody,304634).

This highlights the evolving methods of influence and cross-border ideological propagation within the right-wing ecosystem.
